Etched wafer quality scoring

Python, OpenCV, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib. Sep 2026 to present.

Generates simulated etched wafer images with controlled faults such as bent channels, uneven widths and rounded corners. Thresholding, edge detection and Hough line detection measure channel straightness and squareness, and the measures combine into one score per wafer.

Reached 93.3% accuracy and 0.955 ROC AUC on 240 held-out simulated test images, evaluated with confusion matrices and ROC curves.

Concrete strength prediction

Python, TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn, pandas.

A neural network that predicts concrete compressive strength from mix proportions and age, compared against linear regression and random forest baselines on a held-out test set. The network reached R² 0.86 (MAE 4.2 MPa) against 0.51 for linear regression, and the random forest scored 0.90 on the same split.
